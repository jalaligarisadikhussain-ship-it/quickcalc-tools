About QuickCalc

QuickCalc is a lightweight online calculator for addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and percentages. Use the buttons or your keyboard. All calculations happen directly in your browser; no account is needed.

Privacy

This calculator does not send your calculations to a server. If advertising is enabled later, the advertising provider may use cookies or similar technology under its own privacy terms. A complete privacy policy and any consent controls required for your audience should be added before going live with ads.
