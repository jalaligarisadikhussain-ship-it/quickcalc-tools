    About QuickCalc

    QuickCalc offers an accessible basic and scientific calculator. You can enter expressions such as sin(30), sqrt(81), 6nCr2, 2^8, log(100), 3! and (2+3)*4. Switch between degrees and radians, recall your last answer, save a value in memory and convert simple decimals into fractions. All computations run locally in your browser; no account is required.

    Privacy

    This calculator does not send your calculations to a server. If advertising is enabled later, the advertising provider may use cookies or similar technology under its own privacy terms.
